Common questions

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Diction and pick a size. Settings inside the app walks you through it too.

How do I change the theme? Tap the word card. Two themes are free; Diction Pro opens the rest.

How do I cancel or get a refund? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but the app says I am not a member. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.
